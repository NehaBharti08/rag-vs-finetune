# Retrieval recall

Generated by `ragft.retrieval.recall`. **Do not hand-edit.**

Threat 7 in [`../docs/THREATS_TO_VALIDITY.md`](../docs/THREATS_TO_VALIDITY.md):
if retrieval is bad, the RAG arms lose for reasons that have nothing to do with
RAG as an approach. A benchmark that reports "fine-tuning beat RAG" while
quietly running a broken retriever has measured its own bug.

So recall is measured **independently of any arm, before any arm runs**.

recall@5 >= 0.8: retrieval is adequate, so RAG-arm failures are attributable to generation rather than retrieval.

Measured on 281 single-source val questions. Every QA pair records
the section it was generated from, which is ground truth for "did retrieval find
the right place?". 60 multi-hop pairs were excluded because
they cite two sections and "the right place" is ambiguous for them — reported
rather than silently dropped. 30 questions never surfaced their
source section in the top 20.

## The embedder was chosen on this measurement

VidyaRAG embeds with OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small`, which this project cannot
use at zero cost. That forced a choice, so it was made on evidence — on **val**,
**before any arm produced a number** — rather than on taste.

| Model | dim | r@1 | r@5 | time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` | 384 | 0.500 | **0.795** | 128s |
| `BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5` ✅ | 768 | 0.576 | **0.817** | 337s |
| `thenlper/gte-base` | 768 | — | FAILED | — |

`gte-base` crashed inside fastembed with an inhomogeneous-shape error. Recorded
rather than omitted; two candidates were enough to decide.

**The first choice failed the threshold.** `bge-small` measured recall@5 = 0.795,
marginally under the pre-registered 0.80, verdict WEAK. `bge-base` clears it and
improves recall@1 markedly (0.576 vs 0.500), which matters because the top chunk
dominates a 5-chunk context.

**This is not post-hoc tuning.** The mirrored VidyaRAG parameters — chunk 512/64,
top_k 20 → 5, dense only, no reranker — were not touched. The embedder is the one
retrieval component with no upstream value to preserve.
